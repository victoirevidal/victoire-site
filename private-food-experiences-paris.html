Private French Food Experiences

A beautiful living room, a table worth gathering around, and a taste of French hospitality.

Some of the loveliest moments are spent around a table, with good food, interesting company and plenty to talk about.

In the private home of two Parisian gallery owners, antique dealers and collectors, Victoire welcomes guests into a setting filled with paintings, antiques and objects gathered over the years.

Born and raised in Paris, and formerly a professional chef, she brings together two of her favourite things: sharing French food and the pleasure of welcoming people.

Choose between a leisurely apéritif in the collectors' living room or a more hands-on evening cooking and sharing dinner together.

Both experiences are entirely private, relaxed and thoughtfully tailored to the people around the table.

I

A Private Parisian Apéritif

French flavours, beautiful objects and the art of getting together.

A private apéritif in the living room of two Parisian art dealers and collectors, with Champagne, French delicacies and conversations about art and the traditions of French hospitality.

2 hours · 2–6 guests · From €780 for two

The Experience

The afternoon or evening begins with a glass of Champagne and an introduction to the apartment, its paintings, antiques and intriguing objects.

As you settle into the living room, Victoire shares a few stories behind the pieces and the people who collect them. There's room for curiosity, questions and conversation, without turning the occasion into a formal art lecture.

Then comes the apéritif itself: a generous selection of French cheeses, artisanal charcuterie, fresh bread and accompaniments, personally chosen for your gathering.

Over Champagne and wine, discover some of the customs of French entertaining, from the art of setting a table to the little rituals that make an apéritif such a cherished part of everyday life.

There is no formal wine or cheese tasting, no fixed script and no rush. Just a welcoming atmosphere, delicious food and the pleasure of spending time together.

A Possible Encounter with the Collectors

For guests particularly interested in art, the apartment's owners — Parisian gallery owners, antique dealers and collectors — may join the apéritif upon prior request, subject to their availability.

Personalise Your Apéritif

The experience may also be combined with a private gastronomic walk through Paris, with a personalised quotation.

II

Cook, Drink & Dine in a Collector's Home

A private French cooking experience for two, followed by dinner around a beautiful table.

Join Victoire, a Parisian and former professional chef, for a convivial evening of cooking, good wine and conversation in a remarkable private home.

3 hours · Exclusively for 2 guests · From €900

The Experience

Some of the best evenings begin in the kitchen.

Your evening starts with a glass of Champagne and a discovery of the apartment, its paintings, antiques and carefully collected objects. Then, after an introduction to your chosen menu, it's time to cook.

Together with Victoire, you'll prepare a seasonal French starter and main course, sharing a few techniques, stories and conversation along the way.

Whether you love cooking or simply enjoy joining in, the level of participation is entirely up to you. Chop, stir, season and plate as much or as little as you wish.

This is less a traditional cooking class than the pleasure of preparing dinner with friends.

Once everything is ready, the three of you sit down around a beautifully set table to enjoy what you've made, accompanied by French wine.

Dessert comes from a carefully selected Parisian pâtisserie. Because a wonderful French pastry deserves its own place at the table.

Choose Your Menu

A Private Home with a Story to Tell

Neither a restaurant nor a conventional cooking school, this is a genuine private home belonging to two Parisian gallery owners, antique dealers and collectors.

Its living room is filled with paintings, antiques and objects that reflect a shared passion for art and collecting. It's a place with personality, where food, art and conversation feel entirely at home together.

The experiences take place in Levallois-Perret, an elegant residential neighbourhood just beyond Paris's western boundary. Easily reached by taxi or metro, it's around 13 minutes on the direct Line 3 from Saint-Lazare to Anatole France station.

Every gathering is hosted personally by Victoire and adapted to the interests and preferences of her guests.
